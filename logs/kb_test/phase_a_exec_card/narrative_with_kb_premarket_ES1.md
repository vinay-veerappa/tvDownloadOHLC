Thursday of NFP week is about positioning, not committing. The 08:30 claims data will create volatility, but the real event is tomorrow. Here's the priority:

3. **Preferred windows:** The 09:50-10:10 macro window and the 10:00-11:00 Silver Bullet window are where the real move tends to happen after the news dust settles. The 08:30-09:30 window is for watching, not trading.
4. **Size down.** The calendar says 50% of normal. Respect that.
