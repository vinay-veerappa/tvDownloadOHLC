# PRE-SESSION: OVERNIGHT ASSESSMENT (17:00-19:30 ET)
## What to check before Asia opens

**From the previous NY session:**
- How did NY PM manipulate NY AM?

  - **Partial Up** (40.6%) → PM swept AM high → mildly bearish for Asia
  - **Partial Down** (31.7%) → PM swept AM low → mildly bullish for Asia
  - **Inside** (24.0%) → No manipulation → neutral
  - **Engulfs** (3.7%) → Swept both → chop/expansion

**Asia reversal of PM manipulation:**
- Bullish PM manip (Partial Up) → Asia reverses **44.9%**
- Bearish PM manip (Partial Down) → Asia reverses **55.3%**
- **Asia is rarely a reversal session.** It tends to follow the immediate trend or consolidate.
