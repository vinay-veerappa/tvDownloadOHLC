# 6AM Reversal Strategy: Verification Result

## 1. The Strategy
**The Claim**: The 6AM (EST) H1 candle predicts the day's direction based on interaction with Yesterday's High/Low.
- **Sweep & Close Inside** = Reversal (Fade)
- **Break & Close Outside** = Continuation (Trend)

**The Test**:
- **Data**: NQ1 & ES1 (Jan 2023 - Present)
- **Levels**: Prior Day RTH High/Low (09:30-16:15)
- **Win Condition**: NY Session (09:30-16:00) closes in predicted direction.

## 2. Key Findings (NQ1)

### ✅ The "Dip Buy" is Real (71% WR)
When 6AM sweeps **Yesterday's Low** but closes back inside (Reversal Long Signal):
- **Win Rate**: **71.4%** (21 samples)
- **Avg Outcome**: +64 points (Open to Close)
- **Verdict**: **STRONG EDGE**. Buying the 9:30 Open after a 6AM Low Sweep is highly profitable.

### ❌ The "Top Fade" is a Trap (20% WR)
When 6AM sweeps **Yesterday's High** but closes back inside (Reversal Short Signal):
- **Win Rate**: **20.7%** (29 samples)
- **Verdict**: **FADE AVOID**. In the recent bull market (2023-2025), a high sweep usually signals **continuation** upward, not a reversal. Shorting this "fakeout" gets run over 80% of the time.

### ⚖️ Breakouts (Trend Signals)
- **Break High**: 43% Win Rate (Long). 
- **Break Low**: 47% Win Rate (Short).
- **Verdict**: **NOISE**. Closing outside the range at 6AM does not reliably predict the day's trend on its own.

Your friend is partially right. The **6AM Reversal Signature** works consistently for **finding the low of the day** (Dip Buying).
