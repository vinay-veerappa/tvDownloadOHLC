## 1. Macro & Weekly Context Rules

### 1. Daily Candle Science (3-Candle Lookback)
- Analyzes daily candle patterns over 3-candle lookback windows.
- Calculates **30th, 50th, and 70th percentile** MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion) and MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion) distribution boxes.
- *Stat Rule*: 70% of the time, price reverses before exceeding the 70th percentile box; only 30% of the time does it expand cleanly through.

### 2. Weekly Candle Structure
- **Monday/Tuesday Rule**: Statistically, the high or low of the weekly candle is established on **Monday or Tuesday**.
- **Thursday/Friday Rule**: The opposite extreme of the weekly candle is established on **Thursday or Friday**.
- *Macro Application*: If Monday/Tuesday establishes the weekly low, Thursdays/Fridays carry a strong statistical edge to expand and lock in the weekly high.

## 2. Advanced Trade Setups

### 1. The False (Mean Reversion) Setup (60% – 70% Probability)
- Price breaks out of the 7:30–8:30 AM box first, but subsequently reverses before 11:30 AM to sweep the opposing side of the range.
- **Short False (Down-to-Up)**: Price breaks below the 7:30–8:30 AM low first (tapping a lower target between 9:30 and 10:15 AM), then reverses up towards P12 High, London O/U, or Midnight Open.
- **Long False (Up-to-Down)**: Price breaks above the 7:30–8:30 AM high first, puts in a daily high near open, and reverses into an aggressive sell-off.

### 2. The True (Directional) Setup (20% – 40% Probability)
- Clean breakout of the 7:30–8:30 AM box that continues directionally without returning to the opposing side.
- *Continuation Filter*: Verified if price breaches **40 basis points** past the 06:00–09:00 AM range extreme and fails the 4-step reversal counter.

### 3. The Firecracker Setup
- Occurs when high-probability statistical levels (Midnight Open, P12 Mid, London O/U, Settlement) are heavily **stacked on one side** of price.
- Price prints a brief initial wick at the open, then explodes with violent momentum ("firecracker") to sweep and retrace all stacked levels in rapid succession.

### 4. The "Spongebob Foaming at the Mouth" Setup
- High-conviction mean reversion trade occurring when price opens directly on top of extreme lower or upper bounds (Settlement, Globex Open, Asia O/U) while out-of-stat targets sit on the opposite side.
- Offers massive Risk-to-Reward for a mean reversion push back to P12 Mid.

### 5. The 1800 Break / Magic Hour Setup
- Automated play targeting the 18:00–19:30 PM Asia opening drive, capturing the statistical edge of the initial Asian session breakout establishing a valid daily wick.

## 3. Institutional Execution & Risk Management Protocols

### 👑 1. "Cover the Queen" Rule (Core Cash Flow Protocol)
- Upon trade entry, immediately scale out a portion of the position (e.g., 50% or more) at a short distance (**5 to 10 basis points**).
- Locks in cash flow, covers the stop-loss risk, and turns the remaining contracts into a **risk-free trade** ("getting paid to be wrong").

### 🧩 2. Scaling Reversals in Fourths (1/4 Probing)
- Fading momentum ("buying a falling knife") carries high risk. Never enter with full position size at once.
- Break position into **fourths (1/4 size)**, adding size only as each of the **4 Reversal Steps** is confirmed. If stopped early, the loss is capped at a fraction of a full risk unit.

### ⏰ 3. Expiration Date of Statistics (10:15 / 10:30 AM Cutoff)
- Statistical levels like Midnight Open or P12 Mid have strict "expiration dates."
- If live price makes no clear attempt to retrace to them by **10:15 or 10:30 AM**, their statistical probabilities expire ("get shredded"). The day transitions from a False (mean-reversion) to a True (trend expansion) day.

### 🎯 4. RTH Open Acceptance Rule
- Above the 09:30 AM open box, maintain a bullish bias; below it, maintain a bearish bias.
- Never attempt to short above 09:30 or buy below 09:30 without verified structural invalidations.
