# 🗓️ Weekly Candle Structure & Multi-Expiry Expected Move Methodology

---

## 1. Day-of-Week Macro Structural Cycle
Empirical research over thousands of weekly candles reveals a consistent structural sequence:
* **Monday / Tuesday (Formation Phase)**:
  * **80%+ of the time**, either the **Weekly High** or **Weekly Low** is established on Monday or Tuesday.
  * The market spends early week auctions testing liquidity and establishing one boundary of the weekly candle.
* **Wednesday (Mid-Week Transition)**:
  * Retests the weekly midpoint (W-Mid) or consolidates inside Mon/Tue extremes.
* **Thursday / Friday (Expansion Phase)**:
  * Statistically expands away from the Mon/Tue extreme to print the **opposite extreme of the weekly candle** into Friday settlement.
  * *Example*: If the Weekly Low is established on Tuesday, Thursday and Friday carry a heavy statistical edge to expand upward and print new weekly highs.

---

## 2. Multi-Expiry Expected Moves (EM)
* Computed for all available expirations from **0DTE (today) up to and including Next Friday**.
* **Formula**: $\text{EM} = \text{Spot} \times \sigma_{\text{annual}} \times \sqrt{\frac{\text{DTE}}{252}}$
* Provides the implied standard deviation boundaries for risk management and option strike placement.
