## Known Historical Gaps

Historical daily and weekly data is contiguous up to the current date. The only gaps detected during audits perfectly align with known, multi-day **US Market Closures** and historical artifacts.

- **September 2001 (144-168 hours)**: The week-long closure following the 9/11 attacks. (Impacts SPX, NQ1, QQQ, SPY, VIX).
- **December 2006 / January 2007 (120 hours)**: The closure for the National Day of Mourning for President Gerald Ford combined with the New Year's Day holiday.
- **October 2012 (120 hours)**: The closure due to Hurricane Sandy.
- **Older SPX Gaps (1940s-1960s)**: Scattered 120-hour gaps corresponding to historical holiday schedules, wartime operations, and long weekend market closures before modern market schedules were standardized.
- **VVIX (Volatility of Volatility)**: Contains missing data points/gaps clustered in its early inception years (2006) and a few in 2013-2014.
