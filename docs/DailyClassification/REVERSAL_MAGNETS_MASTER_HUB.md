## 🔍 Key Insights by Category

### 1. Round Numbers (The Dow Anomaly)
While all indices respect psychological levels, **YM1 is in a league of its own**. Nearly **40%** of all Dow reversals happen exactly at a '00' or '50' level.
*   **Actionable Advice**: For YM, *always* front-run or target the nearest 50-point increment.

### 2. Time Structures (1H Open)
The **1H Open** (Top of the Hour) is a massive, previously hidden driver for **ES1** and **NQ1**.
*   **Definition**: The Open Price of the **current hourly candle**.
    *   If reversal is at **09:45**, the magnet is the **09:00 Open**.
    *   If reversal is at **10:15**, the magnet is the **10:00 Open**.
*   **ES1**: Time Structures (18.5%) are the second biggest driver.
*   **Actionable Advice**: If trading ES/NQ, pay close attention to the clock. Reversals stick to the hourly open prices.

### 3. Session Levels (The Universal Law)
**Session Levels** (07:30 Open, London High/Low, Midnight Open) are the most consistent driver across all indices (except YM, where Round Numbers dwarf them).
*   **Dominance**: Accounts for ~20-25% of reversals on NQ, ES, and RTY.
*   **Actionable Advice**: The **07:30 Open** remains the single most important specific price level to watch on NQ and ES.

### 4. The "Unexplained" Gap (RTY)
**RTY1** remains the most difficult to predict, with **30.7%** of reversals having no clear technical reason (compared to just 4.4% for YM).
*   **Conclusion**: RTY is less suitable for "precision" reversal trading compared to YM or NQ.
