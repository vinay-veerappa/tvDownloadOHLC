# 🎲 Herman Statistical Boundary Probabilities

> **Core Concept**: Statistical probability cones, volatility dispersion envelopes, and standard deviation outlier boundaries.

---

## 1. The Herman Cones
* **1 Standard Deviation Cone (68.2% Boundary)**: The expected inner distribution boundary for normal market sessions.
* **2 Standard Deviation Cone (95.4% Boundary)**: The outlier boundary. Reaching 2-SD triggers high-probability mean-reversion.
