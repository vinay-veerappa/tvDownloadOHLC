# 🌐 NQStats ALN (Asia / London / NY) Session Dynamics Guide

> **Core System**: NQStats Morning Bias & Overnight Session Structure
> **Data Scope**: 10-Year & 20-Year Empirical High/Low Distributions on NQ

---

## 1. The 4 Canonical ALN Patterns
ALN categorizes the relationship between the Asia session range (18:00–02:00 / 18:00–19:30) and the London session range (02:30–03:30 / 03:00–08:00):

1. **`LPEU` (London Protrusion Expansion Up)**:
   * London creates a protrusion above Asia High and expands higher.
   * **Bias**: Strong Bullish. High probability of NY breaking London High (>75%).
2. **`LPED` (London Protrusion Expansion Down)**:
   * London creates a protrusion below Asia Low and expands lower.
   * **Bias**: Strong Bearish. High probability of NY breaking London Low (>75%).
3. **`LEA` (London Expansion Asia)**:
   * London expands beyond both sides of Asia.
   * **Bias**: Volatility Expansion / Choppy structure.
4. **`AEL` (Asia Expansion London)**:
   * Asia completely engulfs London (London remains inside Asia range).
   * **Bias**: Compression / Coiled.

---

## 2. Broken vs. Held Status (Volatility Multiplier)
* **Held / Held**: Neither Asia nor London extreme was broken pre-market $\rightarrow$ Clean directional structure.
* **Broken / Broken**: Both Asia and London were swept $\rightarrow$ High volatility / Goalpost setup.
