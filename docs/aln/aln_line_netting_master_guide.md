## 1. The ALN Netting Principle
Instead of treating support and resistance as single lines, ALN defines **Order Flow Netting Clusters**:
* **Netting Ceiling**: Liquidity pools where buying momentum exhausts into passive limit sellers.
* **Mid-Net Equilibrium**: The central netting zone where institutional order books cross and rebalance.
* **Netting Floor**: Liquidity pools where selling pressure meets aggressive institutional absorption.
