# 🏛️ Higher Timeframe (HTF) Macro Levels & Excursion Guide

> **Core Philosophy**: "Macro anchors are the institutional balance points. When price trades extended from weekly and monthly equilibriums, mean-reversion gravity intensifies."

---

## 1. Prior Monthly Midpoint (Monthly Mid)
* **Formula**: $\text{Monthly Mid} = \frac{\text{Prior Month High} + \text{Prior Month Low}}{2}$
* **Function**: 50% structural balance point of the previous calendar month.
* **Order Flow Rule**: Holding above Monthly Mid maintains monthly bullish institutional order flow; accepting below shifts macro bias to distribution.

---

## 2. NFP (Non-Farm Payroll) Benchmark Midpoint
* **Definition**: 50% midpoint of the first-Friday economic release daily candle.
* **Formula**: $\text{NFP Mid} = \frac{\text{NFP High} + \text{NFP Low}}{2}$
* **Function**: Serves as a month-long institutional liquidity benchmark and dynamic support/resistance shelf.

---

## 3. Weekly EMA(5) Excursions & 2%-3% Magnet Zones
* **Baseline**: 5-period Exponential Moving Average on completed weekly bars (W-FRI).
* **52-Week Distributions**:
  * **Median Dup (Upper Excursion)**: `+2.36%`
  * **Median Ddn (Lower Excursion)**: `-2.13%`
* **The 2%-3% Zone Rule**:
  * When price extends **2.0% to 3.0%** away from Weekly EMA(5), historical probability of mean-reversion back toward the EMA exceeds **90%**.
