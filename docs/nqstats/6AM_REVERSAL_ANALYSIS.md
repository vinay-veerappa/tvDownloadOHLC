# 6AM Reversal Signature: Claim vs. Reality

## 1. The Claim (Source: Institutional Theory)
**Theory**: The 6AM EST candle (H1, 06:00-07:00) reveals institutional intent for the day.
- **Mechanism**: Institutions use the pre-market to set up the day's move.
- **Reference Levels**: Yesterday's High and Low.
- **Signals**:
    - **Reversal (Fakeout)**: Price sweeps the level but closes back inside. (e.g., Sweep High + Close Low = Short).
    - **Continuation (Trend)**: Price breaks the level and closes outside with momentum.

**Promise**: "14 out of 15 days called correctly."

---

## 2. Verification Methodology
- **Asset**: NQ1 & ES1 Futures (1-Minute Data).
- **Period**: January 2023 - Present (Bull Market Regime).
- **Levels Used**: Prior Day RTH High/Low (09:30-16:15 EST).
- **Signal Logic**:
    - **Long Reversal**: 6AM Candle Low < Prior Low AND Close > Prior Low.
    - **Short Reversal**: 6AM Candle High > Prior High AND Close < Prior High.
- **Win Condition**: NY Session (09:30 Open to 16:00 Close) moves in the predicted direction.

---

## 3. The Results (Evidence)

### ✅ The "Dip Buy" is Real (71% WR)
When 6AM sweeps **Yesterday's Low** but closes back inside (Reversal Long Signal):
*   **Win Rate**: **71.4%** (21 samples on NQ)
*   **Avg Outcome**: **+64.6 points** (Open-to-Close)
*   **Verdict**: **VALIDATED**. This is a high-probability setup. "Buying the Dip" when pre-market raids yesterday's low is a dominant strategy in this regime.

### ❌ The "Top Fade" is a Trap (20% WR)
When 6AM sweeps **Yesterday's High** but closes back inside (Reversal Short Signal):
*   **Win Rate**: **20.7%** (29 samples on NQ)
*   **Verdict**: **BUSTED**. Fading highs in a strong bull market is a losing strategy. A sweep of the high usually led to **continuation** (prices going higher in NY), not a reversal.

### ⚖️ Breakouts (Trend Signals)
- **Break High**: 43% Win Rate (Long). 
- **Break Low**: 47% Win Rate (Short).
- **Verdict**: **NOISE**. Closing outside the range at 6AM did not reliably predict the day's trend on its own.

### 📈 Long-Term Context (2008-Present)
Expanding the view to 17 years (4,400+ days) reveals a deeper truth about market structure.

| Signal | Ticker | Win Rate | Avg Outcome | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sweep Low (Long)** | **NQ** | **55.3%** | **+16.8 pts** | ✅ **Consistent Edge** |
| Sweep Low (Long) | ES | 54.6% | +4.5 pts | ✅ Mild Edge |
| Break High (Long) | NQ | 55.8% | -2.6 pts | ✅ Momentum Works |
| Break High (Long) | ES | 55.3% | -0.3 pts | ✅ Momentum Works |
| Sweep High (Short) | NQ | 43.7% | +11.2 pts | ❌ Shorts Lose |
| Sweep High (Short) | ES | 48.0% | -0.6 pts | ❌ Coin Flip |

**Key Insight**:
Over the long run, the market is structurally bullish.
1.  **Buying Dips** (Sweep Low) works (55% WR).
2.  **Buying Breakouts** (Break High) ALSO works (56% WR).
3.  **Shorting** (Sweep High) fails (43% WR). Even when you get a "Reversal Signal" at the highs, the market tends to drift higher on average (+11 pts).

**The "Recent Regime" (2023-2025)** has amplified the **Dip Buy** edge to **71%**, likely due to the strong trending nature of the current market.

---

## 4. Conclusion & Action Plan
The claim is **partially true** but dangerous if applied blindly to both sides.

**The Golden Setup**:
1.  **Wait for 6AM EST**.
2.  **Check if Yesterday's RTH Low was swept**.
3.  **If Price Reclaimed the Low (Closed back inside)**:
    *   **BIAS**: **BULLISH** for the day.
    *   **ACTION**: Look for Long entries at 09:30 Open.

**The Trap to Avoid**:
*   Do **NOT** short a 6AM sweep of the High. The market is likely to continue higher.
