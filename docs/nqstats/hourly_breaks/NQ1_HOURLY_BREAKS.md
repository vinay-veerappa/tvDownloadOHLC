## IV. Tactical Analysis & Hour-Specific Findings

### A. Trend Persistence Leaderboard
Best hours for continuation plays (Breaking the bias-side level):
1. **09:00** (82.1%)
2. **03:00** (76.7%)
3. **08:00** (74.4%)

### B. The 09:00 Volatility Trap
The 09:00 hour has a unique characteristic: **Double Break Probability is 39.2%**.
Even if you open above the midpoint, there is a massive chance (>40%) that specific hour will break BOTH the Previous High AND Previous Low.
Tactical Implication: Prev Hour Mid is a weak filter for 09:00. Expect expansion.

### C. 10:00 AM Reversal Risk
10:00 AM shows a higher Reversal Probability (**25.6%**) compared to adjacent hours.
Opening above the 09:00 mid does NOT guarantee a breakout. 10am often sets a trap High and reverses.
