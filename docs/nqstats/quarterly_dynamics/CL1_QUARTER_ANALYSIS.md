# Quantitative Analysis of Intrahour Price Boundary Formation: CL1

> **Abstract:** This study examines the probabilistic distribution of daily High and Low price formation across sixty-minute temporal clusters, specifically focusing on the first fifteen-minute interval (Q1). The objective is to determine the statistical significance of initial momentum as a range-boundary predictor.

## I. Methodology & Data Infrastructure
### 1. Data Source and Resolution
- **Instrument:** CL1 (Continuous Futures Contract)
- **Resolution:** 1-Minute OHLC Intervals
- **Temporal Scope:** 2008 - 2026
- **Exclusion Criteria:** Partial sessions and non-trading holidays excluded by data provider defaults.

### 2. Time-Series Alignment
- **Primary Timezone:** America/New_York (EST/EDT)
- **Preprocessing:** Source UTC timestamps localized to Exchange Time to account for RTH/Globex session boundaries.
- **Observation Window:** 18:00 (Session Open) to 16:00 (Session Close).

### 3. Statistical Categorization (Quarters)
The 60-minute hour is discretized into four equal 15-minute segments:
- **Q1 (Initial Quarter):** Minutes :00 - :14
- **Q2:** Minutes :15 - :29
- **Q3:** Minutes :30 - :44
- **Q4:** Minutes :45 - :59

### 4. Mutual Exclusivity Logic for Q1 Influence
To ensure statistical integrity, Table 3 utilizes a mutually exclusive classification system:
- **Q1 High Only:** Q1 interval contains the final hourly High; Low is established later.
- **Q1 Low Only:** Q1 interval contains the final hourly Low; High is established later.
- **Q1 Both:** Both High and Low are established within the Q1 window (Range Contained).
- **Neither (Expansion):** Both High and Low are broken later in the hour (Range Expansion).

## III. Tactical Implications & Expert Analysis
Synthesis of quantitative findings for active market participants:
- **The 'Judas' / Range Setting Hour:** 16:00 has a 71.2% probability of Q1 setting at least one boundary. In this hour, Q1 is highly influential.
- **Trending / Expansion Hour:** 09:00 shows the highest 'Neither' probability (22.3%), meaning price often breaks BOTH the Q1 High and Low, expanding its range significantly later in the hour.
