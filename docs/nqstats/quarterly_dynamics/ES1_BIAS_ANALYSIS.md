## IV. Extended Analysis & Tactical Findings

### A. Most Responsive Hours
Hours where the Prev High/Low + Midpoint bias leads to the cleanest Q1-Q4 trend continuation.
1. **02:00** (23.8%)
2. **01:00** (23.7%)
3. **19:00** (23.5%)

### B. Reversal Danger Zones
Hours where Q1 frequently sets the *opposite* extreme, signaling a failure of the previous hour's bias.
1. **16:00** (67.4%) - Frequent trend exhaustion.
2. **18:00** (61.0%)
3. **22:00** (47.8%)

### C. The 16:00 Close Effect
At 16:00, strong continuation (closing beyond the previous hour's extreme) occurs **26.8%** of the time.
This confirms the strong persistence of trend into the close despite frequent Q4 volatility.
