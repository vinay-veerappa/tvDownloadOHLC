## 2. Statistical NQStats Concepts (RTH & 6AM)

### RTH Breaks & Gaps (Probability for 09:30 Open)
*   **Open Inside Previous RTH Range**: **74.0%** chance to break at least one side. Only **8.3%** chance of an "Outside Day" (both sides).
*   **Gap Up (Open > pRTH High)**: **69.9%** probability of a bullish close above the prior day's high.
*   **Gap Down (Open < pRTH Low)**: Favors continuation lower (Distribution: 26% Gap Up, 15% Gap Down).
*   **Safety Rule**: **84%** probability the *other side* of the prior range remains **SAFE** for the day if a gap occurs outside.

### 6AM Reversal Signature (Pre-Market Catalyst)
*   **Sweep Low (Long)**: Sweep of yesterday's RTH Low at 06:00-07:00 ET. If price closes back inside $\rightarrow$ **71% WR** for a Bullish NY session.
*   **Sweep High (Short)**: Sweep of yesterday's RTH High. **WARNING**: **20% WR** (Busted). In current regime, High sweeps usually lead to continuation, not reversal.

## 3. ICT Smart Money Concepts (Engine Specs)

### Core Delivery Triad
1.  **Internal Imbalance (FVG/OB)**: If filled $\rightarrow$ Next draw is **External Liquidity**.
2.  **External Liquidity (BSL/SSL)**: If swept $\rightarrow$ Next draw is **Internal Imbalance**.
3.  **Narrative**: Narrative is determined by whether price is in **Premium** (>50%) or **Discount** (<50%) of the current dealing range.

### Structure & Signatures
*   **CISD (Change in State of Delivery)**: High-probability trend shift confirmed when a FVG or OB is completely violated.
*   **TTrade Fractal**: Mechanical 4-candle model (C1-C4) for confirming market entry at HTF levels.
*   **PO3 (Power of 3)**:
    *   **Accumulation**: Near Midnight Open.
    *   **Manipulation**: Judas Swing.
    *   **Distribution**: Expansion session.
*   **Execution Zone**: Buy in **Discount** (below Midnight Open), Sell in **Premium**.

### 4.2 The 3 Market Modes
*   **Expansion Mode (09:00 AM)**: High/Low often forms in **Q4** (last 15m) ~40% of the time. ORB WR is low (54.3%). Expect late-hour continuation.
*   **Reversion Mode (10:00 AM - 02:00 PM)**: High/Low often forms in **Q1** (first 15m). **Fade early moves**. Q1 Highs result in a **RED close 85% of time**.
*   **Trend Close (03:00 PM)**: Power Hour typically expands late. **41.5%** chance the Hour High forms in Q4. Do not fade the final drive.

### The 5-Minute ORB Prediction
Wait for the first 5 minutes of any hour:
*   **Green 5m ORB**: **71% Probability** that the High forms **LATE (Q2-Q4)**. (Expansion/Trend Day).
*   **Red 5m ORB**: High likely forms **EARLY (Q1)**. (Reversion Day, Fader).

### Multi-Hour Windows
*   **06:00 - 10:00 (Pre-Open)**: Extremely back-weighted expansion. 46% chance H/L forms in 09:00 hour. Do NOT fade the 4H range extremes at the 09:30 open.
*   **10:00 - 14:00 (Prime Session)**: Front-weighted reversion. 42% chance the absolute H/L forms in the 10:00-11:00 hour.

### 4.1 Initial Balance (IB) — [09:30 - 10:30 ET]
*   **The 96% Rule**: **96.1% probability** either the IB High or IB Low is broken before 16:00.

*   **IB Midpoint Bias**: If 10:30 candle closes in the **Upper 50%** of IB $\rightarrow$ **82.3%** High break probability. (If Low set first $\rightarrow$ **84.0%**).

### 4.2 Noon Curve (High/Low Distribution)
*   **The Opposite Edge**: **72.8%** probability that the session High and Low form on **opposite sides** of Noon (12:00 PM).
*   **Timing Rule**: If the High is set between 08:30 and 11:00 AM, the edge favors a new low in the afternoon (14:00-15:30).

### 4.4 1H Continuation — [The 9 AM Anchor]

### 4.5 Hour Reversion Rule (9 AM Peak)

### 4.6 Hourly Midpoint & Quarters

## 6. Session Profiler Logic (Structural Outcome)
The Profiler classifies market behavior based on how price interacts with specific time-based "Boxes". This logic is independent of daily bias and focusing on **Breakout Direction** vs **Holding Power**.

### 6.1 Profiler Session Boxes (ET)
| Session | Box Start | Box End | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asia** | 18:00 | 19:30 | Initial overnight range. |
| **London** | 02:30 | 03:30 | European open testing Asia. |
| **NY AM** | 07:30 | 08:30 | US Pre-market header. |

### 6.2 Status Definitions (The 4 Quadrants)
| Status | Sentiment | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long True (LT)** | Strong Bullish | Break Session High AND **Hold** Session Low. |
| **Short True (ST)** | Strong Bearish | Break Session Low AND **Hold** Session High. |
| **Long False (LF)** | Reversal (Short) | Break High THEN Break Low (Fakeout High). |
| **Short False (SF)** | Reversal (Long) | Break Low THEN Break High (Fakeout Low). |
| **None (Range)** | Neutral | Price remains inside the session range. |

### 6.3 "Broken" vs "Held" Logic
A secondary condition determining if a session's range remains valid support/resistance.
*   **Condition**: Price touches the **Session Midpoint** `(High + Low) / 2`.
*   **Timing Constraint**: The "Broken" check begins **strictly after** the next session starts (to avoid self-referencing).
*   **Significance**: A **Held LT** or **Held ST** is a higher conviction trend signal than a **Broken** one.

## 7. Data Integrity & Synchronization
### 7.1 Data Sources & Storage
| Layer | Location | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Live Storage** | `data/live/live_storage_*.parquet` | Real-time 1m streaming buffer. Bridged on startup. |
| **1m History** | `data/*_1m.parquet` | Deep historical minute data. Not updated by live spokes. |
| **HTF History** | `data/*_1d.parquet`, `*_1W.parquet` | Daily/Weekly anchors. Updated by spokes on startup. |

### 7.2 Synchronization Protocol
Before performing any statistical analysis (NQStats/Profiler), verify data parity:
1.  **Check Live Gap**: Last timestamp in `live_storage` must be within 15 minutes of current time during active sessions (18:00 - 17:00 ET).
2.  **Verify Spoke Status**: Ensure `stream_chart.py` is running and the Hub is connected.
3.  **Bridge Gaps**: If the gap exceeds 3 days, the 3-day bootstrap in `stream_chart.py` will leave a hole. Manual sync or increasing bootstrap period is required.

## 8. Timezone Protocol
This protocol is the absolute rule for all time-based calculations and data processing.
| Context | Timezone | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

### 8.1 Critical Handling Rules

- **Session Breaks**: Midnight Open (00:00) and RTH Open (09:30) are strictly in **New York** time.

## 9. Data Storage Architecture (ADR-001)
### 9.1 Separation of Live and Historical Storage
*   **Rule**: Streaming real-time minute data is stored **ONLY** in `data/live/live_storage_*.parquet`.
*   **Historical Parity**: `data/*_1m.parquet` files are for deep historical records and are **NEVER** updated by live streaming processes.
*   **Spoke Responsibility**: `stream_chart.py` must bridge gaps in **Live Storage** (minutes) and **HTF History** (1d/1W) upon startup, but ignore 1m historical parity.

### 9.2 Data Fusion Protocol
All analytical services (Profiler, NQStats) must use the `data_loader.py` fusion layer to merge `data/` (History) and `data/live/` (Recent) into a single contiguous timeline.
