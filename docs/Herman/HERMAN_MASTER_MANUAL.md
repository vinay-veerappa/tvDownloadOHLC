## Part 1: Deep Asia–London Liquidity (The Fundamentals)
*Source: 17-Year Statistical Analysis (4,262 Days)*

### Core Concept: "Sweeps Fuel Expansion"
The market seeks liquidity (stops) before making its real move.

### Session Probabilities
| Session (ET) | Metrics | Key Stat | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-London (00:00-02:00)** | Sweeps Asia High: **34.4%**<br>Sweeps Asia Low: **27.3%** | **Rare (1.1% Both)** | The "Dead Zone". It sets the trap but rarely resolves the day. |
| **London (02:00-05:00)** | Sweeps Asia High: **~60%**<br>Sweeps Asia Low: **~50%** | **High Volatility** | The "Expansion Engine". London almost always sweeps one side of Asia (~90% combined). |
| **Combined (Pre+Lon)** | Sweeps Asia High: **65.3%**<br>Sweeps Asia Low: **55.4%** | **Consistent** | By 5 AM, liquidity has been taken. |

### The Continuation Edge
One of the most powerful findings:
> **"If Pre-London sweeps a level, London tends to sweep it AGAIN (Go Deeper)."**
*   If PL Sweeps High → London Sweeps High Again: **77.2%**
*   If PL Sweeps Low  → London Sweeps Low Again: **69.6%**
*   *Modern Verification Update*: On recent data, this continuation signal is even stronger (**90.2%**).

## Part 2: NQ London Playbook (The Core Strategy)
*Source: NQ London Playbook — Decision Tree (719 Days)*

### 1. The Critical Filter: Asia Range Size
Volatility is cyclical. The strategy changes based on how much energy was used in Asia.

| Regime | Metric (Legacy) | Metric (Modern) | Frequency | Character |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small Asia** | < 70.9 pts | **< 0.48%** | **~85-90%** | **Trend Continuation**. Clean moves. |
| **Large Asia** | > 70.9 pts | **> 0.48%** | **~10-15%** | **Mean Reversion**. Choppy, deep stops. |

### 2. The Decision Tree (Small Asia)
> **Condition**: Asia Range < 0.48% (e.g., < 100pts at 20k).

**A. "The Ideal Trend" (PL Inside Asia)**
*   **Setup**: Pre-London stays inside Asia High/Low.
*   **Trigger**: Opening Range (02:00-03:00) Breaks Out.
    *   **OR Breaks HIGH** → **76.5% Bullish Continuation**.
    *   **OR Breaks LOW** → **73.8% Bearish Continuation**.
*   **Execution**: Trade the breakout/retest of OR.
*   **Targets**: Median Penetration **24-30 pts** (Legacy) / **0.15%** (Modern).

**B. "The Reversal" (PL Sweep)**
*   **Setup**: Pre-London sweeps ONE side of Asia.
*   **Trigger**: Opening Range reverses (e.g., PL swept Low, OR breaks High).
*   **Stats**:
    *   **PL Sweeps LOW + OR Breaks HIGH** → **60-70% Bullish Reversal**.
*   **Execution**: Fade the PL Sweep if the OR reclaims the range.

### 3. The Decision Tree (Large Asia)
> **Condition**: Asia Range > 0.48%.

*   **Warning**: Probabilities dilute.
*   **Continuation**: PL High + OR High is **100% Continuation**, but RARE (n=11).
*   **Character**: Extensions are deeper (**38 pts+**) but take longer.

### Trade Management
*   **Entry**: First London Breach + Retest (Highest Quality).
*   **Targets**:
    *   **TP1**: 50% of Median Penetration (~12-19 pts legacy).
    *   **TP2**: 100% of Median Penetration.
    *   **Runners**: Trail once London High/Low is swept.
*   **Time to Resolution**:
    *   Fast Moves: 2-6 minutes.
    *   Slow Moves: 8-15 minutes.

## Part 3: Historical Sweep & Return to Open (Mean Reversion)
*Source: 10-Year Statistical Analysis (4,291 Sessions)*

This study applies to **Any Hourly Range** (e.g., 08:00-09:00 Range).

### The Logic
1.  **Define Range**: Previous Hour High/Low.
2.  **Trigger**: Current Hour sweeps that High/Low.
3.  **Target**: Does price return to the Next Hour's Open?

### Key Stats
*   **Sweep Frequency**: **94.7%** (Hourly ranges almost always get swept).
*   **Return to 3AM Open**: **72.4%** (Specific to London Open).
*   **Return to 50% Range**: **58.4%**.

### Hourly Hotspots (Best for Mean Reversion)
The bar chart reveals specific hours with **>70% Return-to-Open** probabilities:
1.  **08:00 - 09:00 ET** (Pre-NY): **Highest Reversion (~80%)**. If 08:00 sweeps 07:00, it usually returns to 09:00 Open.
2.  **02:00 - 03:00 ET** (London Open): High Reversion (~72%).
3.  **17:00 - 18:00 ET** (Globex Open): High Reversion.

> **Takeaway**: If an hour sweeps the previous hour's high/low during these times, **expect a fade back to the Open**.
