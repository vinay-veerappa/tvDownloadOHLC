# NY AM Session Playbook: Decision Logic & Statistical Framework

**Session Focus**: NY AM (08:00 – 11:00 ET) & The Open (09:30 ET)
**Objective**: Determine the probability of sweeping the London High vs. London Low based on overnight and pre-market context.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: The Hierarchy of Influence

We analyzed 6,000+ days to determine what drives the NY Session direction. The hierarchy of influence is clear and uncompromising:

1.  **Pre-market Action (05:00 – 08:00)**: **DOMINANT**. This is the primary signal. If the Pre-market has already committed to a direction (breaking London High/Low), NY follows it 80%+ of the time.
2.  **London Trend**: **IRRELEVANT** as a standalone signal. A "London Trend Up" does *not* guarantee a NY Bull day if the Pre-market reverses it.
3.  **Asia Size/Trend**: **NOISE**. It dictates volatility but not direction.

**The Golden Rule**: *Trade the chart in front of you (05:00-08:00), not the chart behind you (London/Asia).*

---

## 2. Decision Tree 1: Pre-Market Trend (The "committal")

**Definition**: The Pre-NY Session (05:00–08:00) breaks the London High or Low *before* 08:00.

### Branch A: Pre-NY Breaks London HIGH
*   **Context**: Price expanded above London High during the 05:00–08:00 gap.
*   **08:00 Bias**: **86.4% Bullish** (Expect NY to continue making new Highs).
*   **09:30 Bias**: **76.2% Bullish**.
*   **Playbook**:
    *   **Bias**: Long Only.
    *   **Strategy**: Any dip into the 08:00-09:30 range is a buy. Do not fade the high.
    *   **Trap**: Selling a "London Extension" thinking it's over-extended. It usually isn't.

### Branch B: Pre-NY Breaks London LOW
*   **Context**: Price expanded below London Low during the 05:00–08:00 gap.
*   **08:00 Bias**: **77.9% Bearish** (Expect NY to continue making new Lows).
*   **09:30 Bias**: **68-70% Bearish**.
*   **Playbook**:
    *   **Bias**: Short Only.
    *   **Strategy**: Sell rallies. Valid target is previous day Low or lower.

---

## 3. Decision Tree 2: The "Inside" Day (The Opportunity)

**Definition**: The Pre-NY Session (05:00–08:00) stays **Completely INSIDE** the London Range.
*   **Occurrence**: ~20% of days.

### The Problem
*   **08:00 Bias**: **50% High / 50% Low**.
*   **09:30 Bias**: **48% High / 48% Low**.

### The Solution: The "London Mode" Filter
When Pre-Market is Inside, does the *London Trend* help us?
*   **London Trend UP** + Pre-NY Inside: **47% High / 50% Low**. (Slight Bearish Lean? Reversal/Pullback).
*   **London Trend DOWN** + Pre-NY Inside: **47% High / 46% Low**. (Complete Coin Flip).
*   **London INSIDE Asia** + Pre-NY Inside: **50% High / 45% Low**.

**Verdict**: NO. Even when London trends, if Pre-Market pauses (Inside), the next move is a coin flip.
**Playbook**:
1.  **Wait for 09:30**. 08:00 is often a fake-out in this scenario.
2.  **Trade the Breakout**: Since the probability is 50/50, you must wait for the **NY Opening Range (07:00-08:00 or 09:30-10:00)** to break.
3.  **Target**: The London Boundary (High or Low) is the magnet. Since we are Inside, we have room to run to the edges.

---

## 4. The 09:30 Open Nuance

Does the "Open" (09:30) reverse the "Pre-Open" (08:00)?

*   **Scenario**: 08:00 Sweeps High -> 09:30 Bias?
    *   If 08:00 Swept High (confirming Pre-NY Trend): 09:30 continues High **88%** of the time.
*   **Scenario**: 08:00 is Inside, then 09:30 Breaks?
    *   This is the "Late Waker". Treat the 09:30 Break as the primary signal.

## 5. Summary Tables

### Probability of NY Taking London High (Bullish Case)

| Pre-NY Context (05-08) | London Trend Context | **08:00 Prob** | **09:30 Prob** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Break HIGH** | Trend Up | **86.4%** | **76.2%** |
| **Break HIGH** | Trend Down (Reversal) | **87.4%** | **77.7%** |
| **Break HIGH** | Inside/Outside | **83-85%** | **74%** |
| **INSIDE** | Trend Up | 47% | 48% |
| **INSIDE** | Trend Down | 48% | 48% |
| **Break LOW** | Any | ~12% | ~20% |

### Key Takeaway
The **Pre-NY Action (05:00-08:00)** is the specific "Setup" for the NY Session. It overrides the broader London Trend.
*   **If 05-08 broke the range**: Follow the break.
*   **If 05-08 is inside**: Wait for the Open.
