# Cross-Ticker Edge Strategy: 2026 Optimization

This strategy document identifies which ticker offers the highest statistical advantage at specific times of the day, based on multi-thousand day research.

---

## 🕒 1. Pre-Market & Early NY (08:00 – 10:30 ET)
**BEST TICKER**: **CL (Crude Oil)**
*   **Why**: CL has the highest probability "First Strike" link between London actions and NY targets (~89% in top scenarios).
*   **Strategy**: Wait for the 08:30-09:30 pre-market sweep. If London already swept Asia Low and price is holding above London Mid, be aggressively bullish toward London High.

---

## 🕒 2. NY Open & Initial Trend (09:30 – 11:30 ET)
**BEST TICKER**: **NQ (Nasdaq)**
*   **Why**: NQ shows the strongest momentum persistence. Its hit rates for London High/Low are extremely clean when aligned with the NY Open (~81%).
*   **Strategy**: Use the NQ morning strike tree. High conviction on London High/Low first strikes.

---

## 🕒 3. NY Lunch Session (12:00 – 13:30 ET)
**BEST TICKER**: **GC (Gold)**
*   **Why**: Gold has a unique "Magnetic High/Low" profile. It hits the Lunch High/Low as magnets during the PM session at the highest rate (51.8% combined).
*   **Strategy**: Scalp the edges of the Lunch range. GC tends to fill its range more reliably during this turnover than the S&P 500.

---

## 🕒 4. Afternoon Trend (13:30 – 16:00 ET)
**BEST TICKER**: **ES (S&P 500)**
*   **Why**: ES is the most stable for trend continuation. Its hit rates for AM High/Low extensions are consistent, and it has the lowest "Judas PM" noise.
*   **Strategy**: Identify the AM Midline. If PM opens and holds one side, trade the continuation toward AM High/Low.

---

## 🕒 5. Asia Transition (19:30 – 02:30 ET)
**BEST TICKER**: **CL (Crude Oil)**
*   **Why**: CL has the highest probability "PDC Drift" (31.9%).
*   **Strategy**: If the market is within 10 ticks of PDC at 19:30, target the PDC for a 00:00 midnight open fill.

---

## 🚀 Recommended Ticker Sequence for a Trading Day:
1.  **08:30 - 10:00**: Scan **CL** for the "London Sweep Reversal."
2.  **10:00 - 11:30**: Switch to **NQ** for the "Morning Momentum Strike."
3.  **12:00 - 14:00**: Scalp **GC** for the "Lunch Magnet Revals."
4.  **14:00 - 16:00**: Trend-trade **ES** for the "PM Continuation."
