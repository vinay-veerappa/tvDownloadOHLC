If the RTH gap aligns with the expected reversal, probabilities spike:

| Scenario | Reversal Rate | Interpretation |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Bearish manip + Gap DOWN ("chasing" the sweep) | **78.4%** | Gap confirms manipulation direction |
| Bullish manip + Gap UP ("chasing" the sweep) | **78.5%** | Gap confirms manipulation direction |
| Bearish manip + Gap UP ("better price") | 60.1% | Gap opposes — weaker |
| Bullish manip + Gap DOWN ("better price") | 59.6% | Gap opposes — weaker |

**The gap that "chases" the manipulation adds 18% to reversal probability.** This is one of the strongest confluence signals.

Judas adds **19.3%** to reversal probability. When present, it's the highest single confidence booster.

# SESSION 4: NY AM (09:30 — 12:00 ET)
## *The reversal window. Execute your plan.*

### Reversal Timing — Every Minute Matters

| Window | Reversals | Cumulative | Action |
|--------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **09:30-10:00** | **60.6%** | 62.3% | PRIME TIME — watch for rejection at MZ array |
| **10:00-10:30** | 12.8% | 74.0% | Silver Bullet window — still high probability |
| 10:30-11:00 | 6.8% | **80.8%** | Window closing — if no entry, reduce conviction |
| 11:00-12:00 | 7.3% | **87.7%** | Late — reversal unlikely. Manage existing only. |
| 12:00+ | 12.3% | 100% | Lunch/PM — do NOT initiate new reversal trades |

### Entry Execution

```
1. WAIT for price to retrace into manipulation zone PD array
2. WATCH for rejection candle (wick in, close out)
3. ENTER on next candle after rejection
4. STOP at rejection wick extreme
5. TARGET 1: 1R (take 50% off — pay yourself)
6. TARGET 2: Move stop to breakeven, run to London extreme or CBDR σ level

Risk: |entry - stop| = rejection wick size (market-determined)
```

### If No Entry by 11:00

The window is 80.8% exhausted. Options:
- **If price is moving in bias direction without an entry:** You missed it. Don't chase.
- **If price is still in the MZ zone:** One more chance, but reduced size.
- **If price is going against the bias:** The 15-20% where the bias was wrong. Walk away.

# SESSION 5: LUNCH (12:00 — 13:30 ET)
## *Protect profits. Do NOT trade.*

### What Happens at Lunch

- 87.7% of reversals are already complete
- Lunch consolidates or slightly retraces the AM move
- Low volume = unpredictable wicks

### If You Have a Runner from AM

- **AM move completed (hit London extreme):** Close everything at lunch. Take profits.
- **AM move underway but not complete:** Hold runner with BE stop. Lunch may chop but PM often continues.
- **AM move failed:** You should have been stopped out already.

### Lunch Range → PM Reference

Track Lunch High and Low. These become the primary PM targets:

# SESSION 6: NY PM (13:30 — 16:00 ET)
## *PM continues AM. Manage or scalp, don't reverse.*

### The Core PM Rule

Only **6.8%** of manipulation reversals happen in PM. PM's job is to continue or consolidate AM.

| AM Was... | PM Continues (same direction) | PM Reverses (opposite) |
|-----------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Bullish** (2,739 days) | Hits AM High: **52.3%** | Hits AM Low: 20.4% |
| **Bearish** (2,426 days) | Hits AM Low: **43.5%** | Hits AM High: 22.7% |

PM continues 2-3x more often than it reverses.
